About Me

My name is Tinovimbanashe Chigwada, but everyone calls me Tino. I am an actuary specialising in risk assessment, financial modeling, and data analysis.

I am passionate about artificial intelligence and its ability to enhance actuarial work and support economic development, particularly in less economically developed countries such as Zimbabwe, where I am from.

I am currently pursuing a Master’s in Artificial Intelligence at Indiana Wesleyan University, with graduation expected in 2027.
