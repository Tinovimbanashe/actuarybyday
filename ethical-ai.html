Why This Matters to Me

APPLICATION

Example: AI Personal Value Statement

Below is an example of a human-centric AI personal value statement that applies the principles above to global economic leadership and policy.

Guiding Perspective

Artificial intelligence and machine learning systems are not neutral or purely technical tools. They operate within economic, political, and historical contexts shaped by colonial legacies, structural inequalities, and persistent global power asymmetries. When applied to global economic governance, AI systems risk amplifying these biases if their data, objectives, and performance metrics reflect the priorities of dominant world powers rather than the lived realities of less economically developed nations.

Core Value

Promote equity, cultural respect, and ethical accountability in the deployment of AI and machine learning within global economic systems so that innovation supports empowerment and inclusion rather than dependency or exclusion.

Ethical Lens

AI-enabled economic development should be context-aware and historically informed, recognising how past power structures influence present-day data, models, and outcomes.

Strategies to Fulfil This Value

01

Acknowledge Structural Bias

Evaluate AI through a historical and structural lens that accounts for global power imbalances embedded in data and model design.

02

Design for Local Context

Reflect local economic conditions, governance structures, and cultural norms through meaningful regional engagement.

03

Balance Decision-Making Power

Ensure less economically developed nations participate in objectives, evaluation metrics, and policy recommendations.

04

Challenge Self-Interest

Make interests embedded in systems for trade, lending, risk assessment, and development planning transparent.

05

Foster Ethical Leadership

Combine technical expertise with lived experience and local knowledge from affected communities.

06

Redefine Success

Measure human well-being, sustainability, resilience, and distributive justice alongside growth and accuracy.

Responsible AI integration acknowledges historical bias, addresses technological power asymmetries, and prioritizes dignity, autonomy, and shared prosperity in global economic decision-making.

CLOSING VIEW

Redefining Success

Growth, optimisation, and predictive accuracy are incomplete measures of progress. Ethical AI leadership prioritises long-term human well-being, trust, and shared prosperity over short-term efficiency gains.
