My AI Framework

Building Intelligent Solutions for Tomorrow

This personal framework reflects my growth as an emerging AI ML leader and captures how my skills, perspectives, and aspirations have evolved through this course. Coming from an actuarial background, I have increasingly leaned into the strategic and human centered dimensions of AI ML.

This framework is intended to guide my decisions, leadership approach, and continued development as I work at the intersection of technology, ethics, and societal impact.

Mission Statement

My mission as an AI ML leader is to responsibly use artificial intelligence to create meaningful, scalable solutions that improve quality of life and reduce human suffering.

I aim to bridge technical capability with ethical leadership by empowering people, strengthening organizations, and ensuring AI enhances rather than diminishes the human experience.

My Leadership Framework

Core Values

Human centered impact
I believe AI should ultimately serve people. Every initiative should be evaluated based on whether it improves well being, preserves dignity, and supports human potential.

Ethical responsibility
Ethical and societal impacts are foundational. I am committed to transparency, fairness, and accountability in AI systems.

Empowerment through learning
Sustainable AI adoption depends on informed and engaged users.

Faith informed integrity
My leadership is grounded in integrity, stewardship, and respect for others.

Key Objectives

  • Assess AI tools for effectiveness, scalability, and fit
  • Lead AI initiatives with focus on adoption and change management
  • Advance expertise through graduate education and economics study
  • Promote ethical and socially responsible AI practices
  • Translate complex AI concepts into practical human-centered solutions

Action Plans

Short term
Completing my master’s program, continuing hands-on AI experimentation, and building tangible projects such as Actuary by Day.

Mid term
Expanding my academic foundation in economics and decision-making, and engaging with AI leaders and professional communities.

Long term
Leading or advising on AI initiatives that address complex societal challenges at the intersection of technology, policy, and ethics.

This framework is not static. It is designed to grow as I grow, providing both direction and flexibility as I continue developing into the kind of AI ML leader I aspire to be.
