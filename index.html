​​My first Chatbot

This project marks the very first artifact on my landing page. An early milestone in my journey into the world of AI. I created it using prompts in ChatGPT. Prior to this assignment, I proudly belonged to the “AI skeptics club,” unsure whether these tools were worth the hype. However, after completing this exercise in my AIML class, my perspective changed almost instantly.

In the span of a few seconds, AI produced coding that would have taken me far longer-an outcome made even more impressive considering my well‑documented dislike of coding. That alone was nearly enough to convert me on the spot. Almost.

In all seriousness, building this bot demystified the process of using AI and showed me just how accessible and powerful these tools can be. What started as an assignment became a moment of curiosity—one that encouraged me to explore more use cases, ask more questions, and appreciate the value of AI in a hands‑on way.

Meet EcoTravel : 

You may already be acquainted with EcoTravel if you clicked the EcoTravel Chatbot button in the home page. But if you have not yet met EcoTravel, say hi using the link below. 

https://chatgpt.com/g/g-69ab1558392c819182df488091009a73-ecotravel-ai

EcoTravel AI is a generative‑AI–powered chatbot designed to help users make more environmentally responsible travel decisions. Built as part of an AI lab project, the bot uses a large language model to answer questions about sustainable tourism, eco‑friendly transportation, responsible travel habits, and ways to minimize environmental impact. It offers recommendations such as choosing greener transportation, reducing waste, supporting local businesses, and selecting eco‑friendly accommodations, all aimed at promoting sustainability‑focused travel behavior.

Important Lessons Learned

The project highlighted several key insights: Prompt quality matters. More precise prompts yielded clearer, more relevant sustainability guidance, while vague prompts led to overly general responses.

AI can effectively structure and simplify complex environmental concepts, making sustainability more accessible to users.

Design thinking is useful in AI development. Applying the Empathize‑Define‑Ideate‑Prototype‑Test framework helped ensure the bot addressed real user challenges in sustainable travel.

Generative AI is a practical tool for real‑world issues. This project demonstrated how AI can support environmental awareness and help users make more informed travel decisions.
